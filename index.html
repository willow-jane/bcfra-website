They run toward the call. We run toward them.

Since 2014, the Brazos County First Responders Association has given financial support to local first responders who are ill or injured in the line of duty, and funded scholarships for their children.

What your support does

Every dollar is raised in Brazos County and stays in Brazos County.

Line-of-duty support

Direct financial help for local first responders who become ill or injured in the line of duty, and for their families.

Scholarships

Scholarships for graduating high school seniors whose parents serve as Brazos County first responders.

A community that shows up

Our Steak Dinner, Chili Cookoff and BBQ Cookoff bring officers, firefighters, families and neighbors to the same table.
