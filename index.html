Founded after we lost one of our own

In 2012, Brazos County lost Constable Brian Bachmann in the line of duty. His fellow officers saw firsthand the immediate needs his family faced in the days that followed, and resolved that no first responder's family here would face that alone again.

The Brazos County First Responders Association was created in 2014. Our board is made up of members of Brazos County law enforcement agencies and the Bryan and College Station Fire Departments.

Constable Brian BachmannBrazos County Precinct 1. End of Watch: August 13, 2012.

What your support does

Every dollar is raised in Brazos County and stays in Brazos County.

Line-of-duty support

Direct financial help for local first responders who become ill or injured in the line of duty, and for their families.

Scholarships

Scholarships for graduating high school seniors whose parents serve as Brazos County first responders.

Nominate a first responder

Know a local first responder facing hardship, illness, injury or crisis? Tell us. Self-nominations are welcome.

  1. Submit the formShare who they are, where they serve, and what's going on.
  2. We verifyWe confirm the nominee's connection to an active first responder agency. This keeps help going where it's meant to.
  3. The board reviewsEvery request is reviewed by our board and handled confidentially.

Submitting a nomination does not guarantee assistance.
