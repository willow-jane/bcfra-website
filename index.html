Where your money goes

To the people who answer when you call 911, and to their families.

When one of ours gets hurt
Injury, illness, a flood, a house fire. We help cover what insurance won't.
Their kids' education
$1,500 Brian Bachmann Scholarships for graduating seniors whose parents serve Brazos County.
When we lose one of our own
Support for the families of first responders killed in the line of duty. It's why we started in 2014.
