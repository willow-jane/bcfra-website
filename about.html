Why we exist

Founded in memory of Constable Brian Bachmann. Run by the first responders of Brazos County.

Founded after we lost one of our own

In 2012, Constable Brian Bachmann was killed in the line of duty. His fellow first responders saw what his family faced. In 2014, they founded this association so the next family wouldn't stand alone.

Our board is drawn from Brazos County law enforcement agencies and the Bryan and College Station Fire Departments.

Constable Brian BachmannBrazos County Precinct 1. End of Watch: August 13, 2012.

Who you've helped

A few of the first responders and families the association has stood behind, as reported by local news.

Trooper Chad Walker's family

After DPS Trooper Chad Walker was killed in the line of duty in 2021, BCFRA presented his family with a $1,000 check through Precinct 1 Constable Jeff Reeves.

25 News, April 2021

Chief Deputy Constable Calder Lively

When an icy crash on Highway 21 during Winter Storm Uri left him injured, BCFRA helped cover expenses, including hotel stays.

25 News, April 2021

Brian Bachmann Scholarships

Two graduating seniors, the children of a College Station Fire battalion chief and a College Station Police lieutenant, each received a $1,500 scholarship toward college and trade school.
