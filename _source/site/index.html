Founded after we lost one of our own

In 2012, Constable Brian Bachmann was killed in the line of duty. His fellow first responders saw what his family faced. In 2014, they founded this association so the next family wouldn't stand alone.

Our board is drawn from Brazos County law enforcement agencies and the Bryan and College Station Fire Departments.

Constable Brian BachmannBrazos County Precinct 1. End of Watch: August 13, 2012.Read his memorial on the Officer Down Memorial Page ↗

Nominate a first responder

Know a local first responder facing hardship, illness, injury or crisis? Tell us. Self-nominations are welcome.

  1. Submit the formShare who they are, where they serve, and what's going on.
  2. We verifyWe confirm the nominee's connection to a first responder agency.
  3. The board reviewsThe board decides how we can help.

We can't fund every request, but we read every one and follow up. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
