Nominate a first responder

Tell us who needs help. The board reviews every request in confidence.

How it works

Know a local first responder facing hardship, illness, injury or crisis? Tell us. Self-nominations are welcome.

  1. Submit the formShare who they are, where they serve, and what's going on.
  2. We verifyWe confirm the nominee's connection to a first responder agency.
  3. The board reviewsThe board decides how we can help.

We can't fund every request, but we read every one and follow up. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
