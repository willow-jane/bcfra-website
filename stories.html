College Station Fire Department · August 2026

Grayson's fight

Grayson Brock grew up around the job. His dad, Steve, retired as a lieutenant with College Station PD. Grayson went through EMT and fire school at TEEX and joined College Station Fire. Three years in, he was diagnosed with stage 3 cancer.

Brazos County showed up. Battalion Chief Jason Giles organized the Brock 5K at Brayton Fire Training Field, a silent auction and a profit share at the Angry Elephant. 371 runners fought the August heat. Firefighters, police, A&M EMS and neighbors raised nearly $23,000 for the Brock family.

The BCFRA board voted to help, then voted to help again. On a visit to MD Anderson in The Woodlands, board members saw what his treatment days looked like: chemo from 6:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., no TV, and light too dim to read by because treatment had made his eyes sensitive.

So the board bought Grayson an iPad to watch TV, read or play games through the long days. Jason delivered it to Steve.

“It takes a village sometimes to come together for the betterment of others.”Battalion Chief Jason Giles, CSFD

Grayson is still in treatment, and he says things are looking positive. Keep him and the Brock family in your prayers.
